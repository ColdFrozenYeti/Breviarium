Breviarium 1.3 adds **two more fonts** and a few fixes from praying with 1.2.

**What's new**

- **Two more fonts**, under *Font*: **Gentium Book Plus**, a second serif, and **Source
  Sans 3**, a second sans serif, each sized to read like Hoefler Text. Both ship in the app
  unmodified under the SIL Open Font License (`NOTICE.md`, and the About screen).
- **SF Pro** now sets antiphons and headings in bold, as the other fonts do; its antiphons
  had come out in regular italic.
- **Settings are regrouped**: *Sacerdos vel diaconus adest*, *Rubricæ*, *English
  translation* and *Psalterium* in one block, and *Text size*, *Theme* and *Font* together
  under *Appearance*.
- On a light theme, a white day's dot in *Jump to date* has a fine **gold edge**, so it no
  longer vanishes into the page.
- IBM Plex Mono and Gentium Book Plus, whose lines are taller, keep Hoefler Text's line
  spacing more closely.

**Still open**

The Ambrosian texts and calendar still wait on the user's check and Church of Ambrose's
review (`docs/ambrosian-compline-checks.md`).

**Installing**

`Breviarium-1.3.ipa` below is **unsigned**. Sign and install it from Windows with
AltStore/AltServer and a free Apple ID, as in `docs/install-on-iphone.md`. The signature
lasts 7 days.
