Breviarium 1.1 is the first bugfix release after 1.0: **Ambrosian Compline as prayed**,
after the user prayed it on the phone on 9 October 2026.

**What's fixed**

- The *Pater noster* and *Ave Maria* at the start are written out in full, and the
  *Pater noster (secreto)* of the *preces* and the conclusion is the whole prayer, as the
  *Credo* is printed. No accents are added to them, as the source adds none.
- The *Gloria Patri* after each psalm is two psalm verses with their asterisks, so it keeps
  the psalm's alternation of upright and italic verses.
- *Salva nos, Domine, vigilantes* with its verses is one antiphon, in bold italic.
- The rubrics the app carries out are no longer shown: the festal rubric and those that
  say when each Marian antiphon is said.
- The signs of the cross Roman Compline has on the texts the two share, which the source
  doesn't print: *Converte nos*, *Deus in adiutorium*, *Nunc dimittis*, *Indulgentiam* and
  *Adiutorium nostrum*.
- Each prayer is its own paragraph, spaced as in the Roman hours; a line with a
  half-verse asterisk is set as a verse wherever it stands; the missing full stops after
  *Amen* are restored.

**Still open**

The Ambrosian texts and calendar still wait on the user's check and Church of Ambrose's
review (`docs/ambrosian-compline-checks.md`).

**Installing**

`Breviarium-1.1.ipa` below is **unsigned**. Sign and install it from Windows with
AltStore/AltServer and a free Apple ID, as in `docs/install-on-iphone.md`. The signature
lasts 7 days.
