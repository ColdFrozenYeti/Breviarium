Breviarium 1.2 adds **colour themes and fonts** (`docs/1.2_plan.md`).

**What's new**

- **Eight colour themes**, chosen in Settings under *Theme*: *Nox* (the black page, still
  the default), *Media nox*, *Carbo*, *Lux*, *Sepia*, *Vellum*, *Silva* and *Rosa*. A light
  theme makes the whole app light, the calendar and the hour picker included.
- **Four fonts**, under *Font*: Hoefler Text (the default), SF Pro, IBM Plex Mono and
  Comic Neue, each sized to read like Hoefler Text. IBM Plex Mono and Comic Neue ship in
  the app under the SIL Open Font License (`NOTICE.md`, and the About screen).
- Both are chosen from a **gallery** like the app icon's: each tile shows Easter Sunday's
  *Magnificat* antiphon and the canticle's opening, set as the office sets them, in that
  theme or font.
- The **page curl** is now the default page turn; the slide is still a choice.
- Jump to date's colour dots have no outline, and a black day's dot is black on a light
  theme.

**Still open**

The Ambrosian texts and calendar still wait on the user's check and Church of Ambrose's
review (`docs/ambrosian-compline-checks.md`).

**Installing**

`Breviarium-1.2.ipa` below is **unsigned**. Sign and install it from Windows with
AltStore/AltServer and a free Apple ID, as in `docs/install-on-iphone.md`. The signature
lasts 7 days.
