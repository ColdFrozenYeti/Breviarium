Beta 3 adds **Matins**, completing the Roman office: Matins, Lauds, Prime, Terce, Sext,
None, Vespers and Compline, under the 1960 rubrics, in Latin with an optional parallel
English, in either the Vulgate or the Pius XII psalter.

**What's new since Beta 2**

- Matins: the invitatory, the hymn, the nocturns with their psalms, lessons and
  responsories, and the *Te Deum*. Nine lessons in three nocturns on I and II class days;
  one nocturn and three lessons on Sundays, III class feasts and ferias, with the 1960
  joining of the second and third lessons. Tenebræ in the Triduum.
- *Ad Matutinum* is first in the hour picker, and the app opens on it from midnight to
  five in the morning.
- Lessons read as prose paragraphs under a small *Lectio i* line; with English on they
  are stacked in portrait and side by side in landscape.
- The line under the day's title: the commemoration (*Commemoratio ad Laudes tantum: …*),
  the season's Scripture at Matins (*Tempora: …*), or a transferred feast, as Divinum
  Officium shows it for each hour.
- A new app icon: an illuminated B.
- Matins is checked against Divinum Officium for every day from 2025 to 2040, in both
  psalters and with the English, plus all of 2044 and the named edge cases, like the
  other hours.

**Installing**

`Breviarium-Beta-3.ipa` below is **unsigned**. Sign and install it from Windows with
AltStore/AltServer and a free Apple ID, as in `docs/install-on-iphone.md`. The signature
lasts 7 days.
