Beta 2 is the **Roman diurnal**: Lauds, Prime, Terce, Sext, None, Vespers and Compline,
under the 1960 rubrics, in Latin with an optional parallel English, in either the Vulgate
or the Pius XII psalter.

**What's new since Beta 1**

- The six day hours, alongside Vespers. The app opens on the hour for the time of day,
  and tapping the hour's name at the top switches hours.
- Every hour is checked against Divinum Officium for every day from 2025 to 2040, in
  both psalters and with the English, plus all of 2044 as a hold-out year and the named
  edge cases (the Triduum, All Souls, Our Lady on Saturday, the Greater Litanies,
  transferred feasts and more).
- Chapters and short lessons show their Scripture reference above the text, like a
  psalm's number, and their response (*Deo grátias*) as an indented italic line, with no
  V./R. marks.
- The Marian antiphon at Compline reads as one stanza, and no "Benedictio" or
  "Absolutio" labels appear in the office.
- Pages: the last page always reaches the end of the hour, and a heading or reference is
  never left alone at the foot of a page.

**Installing**

`Breviarium-Beta-2.ipa` below is **unsigned**. Sign and install it from Windows with
AltStore/AltServer and a free Apple ID, as in `docs/install-on-iphone.md`. The signature
lasts 7 days.
