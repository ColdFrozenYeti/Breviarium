Breviarium 1.4 answers App Review's 4.2 rejection of 1.3 (`docs/1.4_plan.md`): it runs
**natively on the iPad**, adds the **Kalendarium** and makes its **menus** plain to see.

**What's new**

- **The iPad**, natively, in every orientation. In landscape the hour lies open like a
  book, **two pages at a time**: the page curl turns a leaf about the spine, the slide moves
  the whole spread, and page 1's header is on the left page. With English on, the **Latin
  is on the left page and the English on the right**, level unit by unit, like a
  facing-page edition. In portrait, one page as on the iPhone, its line held to about
  34 em. Turning the iPad keeps your place. The iPad has the iPhone's settings and none of
  its own.
- **The Kalendarium**, in place of *Jump to date*: the year as a list of months, one row a
  day with its class, its name (in an Ordo's abbreviations where it is too long, *In
  Comm.*, *Feria V hebd. XXIII post Oct. Pent.*) and its colour, today framed. Every row
  comes from the engine the hours use. Tap a day to open it at the hour already chosen; it
  opens from the new calendar button, the date line and the footer's date.
- **Menus**, under *Appearance*: *Standard* (the default) draws the gear, the hour title
  as a picker with a chevron, the calendar button and the day arrows in the theme's rubric
  colour; *Prayer* keeps the grey look of 1.3; *Hidden* leaves only the prayer and a bare
  page number, and a tap on it or on the top strip brings the menus back until the next
  page turn.
- At Prime, Terce, Sext, None and Compline, the chapter, short responsory and versicle
  are each a section of their own, as at Lauds and Vespers; *Noctem quiétam* and the
  *Amen* after *Misereátur* and *Indulgéntiam* are set as responses; the *Glória Patri*
  after *Deus in adiutórium* keeps its upright and italic halves; a little space before
  every *Orémus*.

**Behind it**

- App CI builds once and runs its UI tests on five runners, iPhone and iPad: about 25 to 35
  minutes instead of 65. The oracle audits take 39 minutes instead of 50.
- The App Store screenshots now include the 13" iPad's, in landscape
  (`scripts/promo/compose.py --ipad`).

**Still open**

The Ambrosian texts and calendar still wait on the user's check and Church of Ambrose's
review (`docs/ambrosian-compline-checks.md`).

**Installing**

`Breviarium-1.4.ipa` below is **unsigned**. Sign and install it from Windows with
AltStore/AltServer and a free Apple ID, as in `docs/install-on-iphone.md`. The signature
lasts 7 days. For App Review, `testflight.yml` uploads 1.4 to App Store Connect.
