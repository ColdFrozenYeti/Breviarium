Breviarium 1.6 adds **reminders to pray the hours** (`docs/1.6_plan.md`): a notification
at a time you choose for each hour, and for the Martyrology, opening that hour when
tapped.

**What's new**

- **Notifications**, a new row in Settings, opens a page of nine rows: *Ad Matutinum*,
  *Ad Laudes*, *Ad Primam*, *Martyrologium*, *Ad Tertiam*, *Ad Sextam*, *Ad Nonam*,
  *Ad Vesperas* and *Ad Completorium*, each with a switch and, when on, its time. The
  same nine whatever the *Ritus* or *Officium*.
- **Everything is off** until you turn an hour on, and only then does iOS ask whether
  Breviarium may send notifications. Refused, or later turned off in iOS Settings, the
  switches go back off, the times are kept, and the page says how to allow them.
- **Suggested times** when an hour is first turned on: Matins 05:00, Lauds 07:00, Prime
  08:00, the Martyrology 08:15, Terce 09:00, Sext 12:00, None 15:00, Vespers 18:00,
  Compline 21:00. Any hour can then take any time of day.
- **A reminder says only the hour's name** (*Ad Vesperas*, *Martyrologium*), with the
  default sound and no badge. A tap opens that hour on the day it arrives, under the
  app's own *Ritus* and *Officium*, as a tap on a widget does.
- **In every build**, the sideloaded one included.
- **Settings is tidier**: *Notifications* and the app icon sit in boxes of their own,
  without headings, and the description under *Menus* is gone.

**Behind it**

- Local notifications only: one repeating calendar notification per hour on, in the
  phone's local time, scheduled by the app itself and checked again at every change,
  at launch and on coming back to the foreground. No server, no push, no entitlement,
  no background mode: App CI checks the app declares neither `aps-environment` nor
  `UIBackgroundModes`, so a free Apple ID still signs it. The privacy manifest doesn't
  change.
- A tap goes through the widgets' link (`breviarium://open?hour=…&date=…`), which now
  also names the Martyrology.
- `NotificationsUITests`, on the iPhone 17 and the iPad Air 11" (M3), through a
  debug-only stand-in for the notification centre: the page all off, some on, a time
  being edited and after a refusal; the suggested times; any time for any hour; a tap
  opening Christmas Lauds and the Martyrology. One test uses the real notification
  centre, accepting iOS's own permission alert.
- App CI's groups are rebalanced: the reminders' tests join Matins on the iPhone and
  the iPad pass, and `GalleryUITests` moves to Matins.
- Version 1.6 (build 7).
