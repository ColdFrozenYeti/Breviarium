Breviarium 1.5 puts the office on the **Home Screen and the Lock Screen** (`docs/1.5_plan.md`):
widgets that show the day and the hour for the time of day, and open it when tapped.

**What's new**

- **The day**, small and medium: the day's colour, its class and its name, as large as
  it fits; the medium one adds the date line and the hour's commemoration line, as the
  page has them.
- **The hour**, medium and large: the hour for the time of day and its antiphon, the
  *Benedictus* antiphon at Lauds, the *Magnificat* at Vespers, the *Nunc dimittis* at
  Compline, the first psalm's at the others, centred and as large as it fits. Where an
  hour has none, as at the little hours of the Triduum, the day's title stands in its
  place. The large one also has the date line, the class, the day's name and the page's
  rule.
- **The Lock Screen**: the day's name and class (rectangular), the hour (inline) and the
  Breviarium *B* in a ring (circular).
- The widgets follow the app's own **Ritus, Officium, Psalterium, Theme and Font**, and
  redraw when one changes. A tap opens that hour on that day.
- The hour changes at 05:00, 09:00, 12:00, 15:00, 17:00 and 20:00, as the app's own
  opening hour does, and the day at midnight.
- On the iPhone and the iPad. Widgets come with the TestFlight and App Store build; the
  sideloaded build has none.

**Behind it**

- The widgets compute the office themselves, from the app's own data, for any date. A
  widget reads its data from a packed file (`breviarium-widget.pack`, 9.18 MB, the
  Latin without the English) one file at a time: a day reads at most 80 of its 3,873
  files. Every run of the data tool checks a day's timeline against the widget's budget
  (2.8 MB and 0.33 s measured, against 10 MB and 2 s).
- The settings reach the widgets through an App Group, in the TestFlight and App Store
  build only. `testflight.yml` signs it in and checks it is there; `build-ipa.yml`
  leaves the widget and the App Group out of the sideloaded `.ipa`, so the free Apple ID
  signs the app alone, as before.
- The widget's antiphons are checked against Divinum Officium's pages, for both
  psalters, and its day against the app's own pages, for both rites.
- A debug-only gallery in the app draws every widget at the device's sizes, for App
  CI's snapshots on the iPhone and the iPad.
- The time-of-day schedule moved into the Kit (`CanonicalHour.forTimeOfDay`), shared by
  the app and the widgets.
