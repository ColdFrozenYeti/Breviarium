Breviarium 1.0 completes the roadmap begun with the alpha: the Roman office under the
1960 rubrics at every hour, the Little Office of Our Lady, the Office of the Dead and the
Martyrology; the Dominican office of 1962; and now **Ambrosian Compline**, from the 1957
breviary.

**What's new since Beta 5**

- **Ambrosian Compline.** Settings → *Ritus* → *Ambrosianus* gives *Completorium
  Ambrosianum*, the Compline of the 1957 *Breviarium Ambrosianum* in its four seasonal
  forms. It follows the Ambrosian calendar (from the 1954 *Missale Ambrosianum*): the
  festal or ferial form, the Marian antiphon of the season, both hymns in Lent, and the
  priest's and the choir's *Confiteor*, or one *Confiteor* without a priest. Latin only.
  The texts are Church of Ambrose's digitisation, used with their owners' permission; the
  About screen credits them.
- **The Ambrosian calendar** names every Sunday and day of the season, follows the
  Missal's rubrics for Sundays, transferred feasts and privileged days, and shows its
  colours in *Jump to date*.
- **English side by side throughout**, but for the Matins lessons: a response no longer
  sits under its own translation twice.
- **More rubrics in red**: *(fit reverentia)* and *(bow head)* in the psalms, the *Te
  Deum*'s English directions, the genuflection lines in hymns such as *Ave maris stella*
  and *Pange lingua*, and *(hic genuflectitur)* in the Dominican Prima's Gospel. A scan of
  every hour of a year, in both rites, finds none left in white.
- **Six app icons**: *Default*, *Benedictus*, *Gutenberg (mono)*, *Gutenberg*,
  *ChurchofAmbrose* and *Weissenau*, in Settings → *App icon*.

**Still open**

The Ambrosian texts and calendar wait on the user's check and Church of Ambrose's review
(`docs/ambrosian-compline-checks.md`); a few calendar questions the sources leave open are
listed there. The release pass planned for 1.0 (every hour on the phone, landscape, the
largest text size) moves to the 2.0 roadmap (`docs/roadmap-2.0.md`).

**Installing**

`Breviarium-1.0.ipa` below is **unsigned**. Sign and install it from Windows with
AltStore/AltServer and a free Apple ID, as in `docs/install-on-iphone.md`. The signature
lasts 7 days.
