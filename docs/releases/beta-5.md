Beta 5 adds **the Dominican office**: Divinum Officium's *Ordo Prædicatorum - 1962*, with
the Order's own calendar, saints, commons, psalter and hymns, at every hour.

**What's new since Beta 4**

- **The rite is a setting.** Settings → *Ritus* → *Dominicanus* chooses the Dominican
  office, with its own *Officium* choice: *Officium diei* (the default), the Little Office
  of Our Lady or the Office of the Dead. *Romanus* is unchanged.
- **All eight hours** follow the Order's books as DO has them: its psalter distribution
  and hymns, its Matins (three lessons or nine, with the Order's responsories and Marian
  blessings), its Prime (the Rule of St Augustine, or the day's Gospel on feasts), its
  Compline and the Order's own commemorations.
- **Both psalters**: the Vulgate by default, the Pius XII psalter as an option, as under
  *Romanus*.
- **The English** is the Order's own where DO has it; otherwise the Roman English of the
  same text. Where the Roman English stands for a Dominican Latin that differs a little
  (a saint's name in a collect), a small grey line under it says *Roman text; the
  Dominican Latin differs.* Where there is no Roman counterpart, the text is Latin only.
- **The Martyrology** under *Dominicanus* is the 1960 Roman one, with the Dominican day's
  title block (DO has no Dominican Martyrology).
- **Settings → App icon** chooses the original icon or the illuminated *B*.
- The Dominican office is checked against Divinum Officium at every hour on every day
  from 2025 to 2040, in both psalters and with the English, plus all of 2044 with the
  priest on and off; its Little Office (2026 and 2027) and Office of the Dead (2026) the
  same way, as for the Roman ones.
  Where DO's Dominican data is in error (a broken reference, a placeholder, a misprint),
  the app shows the corrected text; where the Order's own text is missing from DO, the
  app shows nothing in its place rather than a guess. Each case is listed in
  `docs/rubrics-op1962.md`, §8.

**Installing**

`Breviarium-Beta-5.ipa` below is **unsigned**. Sign and install it from Windows with
AltStore/AltServer and a free Apple ID, as in `docs/install-on-iphone.md`. The signature
lasts 7 days.
