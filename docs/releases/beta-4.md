Beta 4 adds the offices said beside the office of the day: **the Little Office of Our
Lady**, **the Office of the Dead** and **the Martyrology**, with a dot of each day's
**liturgical colour** in the calendar.

**What's new since Beta 3**

- **The office is a setting.** Settings → *Ritus* → *Romanus* chooses *Officium diei* (the
  default), *Officium parvum B.M.V.* or *Officium defunctorum*. The hour picker lists
  that office's own hours; an hour the office hasn't opens the nearest earlier one (the
  Office of the Dead's Terce opens Lauds, its Compline Vespers).
- **The Little Office of Our Lady**, all eight hours, in Divinum Officium's four forms:
  Advent (and the Annunciation), Christmastide to the Purification, Septuagesima to
  Easter (no *Te Deum*), and the rest of the year.
- **The Office of the Dead**: Matins (three nocturns, nine lessons from Job), Lauds and
  Vespers, with *Réquiem ætérnam* after every psalm.
- **The Martyrology**, the last row of the hour picker under every office. It follows the
  traditional use, read at Prime on the eve: a date shows the next day's entry, announced
  with its date in the Roman calendar, the moon's age and the year, then the movable
  feast, the entries and *Et álibi…*. Latin only.
- **Jump to date** is a month grid with a small dot of each day's liturgical colour.
- Directions inside a text, such as *(Fit reverentia)* in the *Te Deum*, are red rubrics,
  hidden when rubrics are off.
- The Little Office (every hour, 2026 and 2027) and the Office of the Dead (2026) are
  checked against Divinum Officium day by day, in both psalters and with the English,
  plus all of 2044 with the priest on and off; the Martyrology against every Prime page
  from 2025 to 2040 and 2044.

**Installing**

`Breviarium-Beta-4.ipa` below is **unsigned**. Sign and install it from Windows with
AltStore/AltServer and a free Apple ID, as in `docs/install-on-iphone.md`. The signature
lasts 7 days.
