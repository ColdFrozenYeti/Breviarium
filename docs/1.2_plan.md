# 1.2: fonts and themes

**Draft, 10 October 2026; every question answered the same day; for the user's approval.** No code is written before it is
approved. 1.2 adds two Settings choices: the **typeface** of the office and a **colour
theme**, each chosen from a gallery like the app icon picker, whose tiles preview the
opening of the *Magnificat* with its antiphon and the red ✠.

## 1. Decisions so far (10 October 2026)

1. **Themes, light ones included.** *Night mode only* was meant for the alpha, to focus on
   the content. The current look becomes **Nox** (the user's *Classic dark*) and stays the default.
   **Eight themes** in all (§3), among them **Media nox** (the user's *Midnight blue*:
   white text, blue rubrics, a very dark navy background), **Lux** (white, black text),
   **Sepia** and **Silva** (light green, black text, dark green rubrics). `CLAUDE.md` is
   amended accordingly.
5. **The theme picker looks like the icon picker** (§4).
6. **Everything ships in the app**: no installed or external fonts (so no Michelangelus,
   even installed on the user's own phone).
7. **One font per category** (§2).
8. **Theme names in Latin**, the Settings page in English: the new rows are *Theme* and
   *Font*.
2. **Fonts**: a choice of typefaces, including a sans serif, a monospaced typewriter face
   and a readable comic face for dyslexic readers (§2).
3. **The App Store release** has no date or number; it comes when the user says so.
4. **Michelangelus can't be bundled.** The user sent it (`Michelangelus_Fonts.zip`), but its
   licence is Microsoft's, and §1(b) says *"No Distribution Rights. This agreement does
   not grant you a license to distribute nor sublicense all or part of the software to any
   third party."* An `.ipa` on GitHub Releases, or on the App Store, is distribution. The
   same holds for Comic Sans. Neither is committed anywhere.

## 2. The fonts

Each font either ships with iOS or carries the SIL Open Font License, which allows
bundling in a GPL app on the App Store (`NOTICE.md` lists each bundled one as third-party,
with its licence). The office needs four styles of each: regular (the text), italic
(responses, rubrics), black or bold (headings, the day title) and bold italic (antiphons).

| Category | Font | Source | Styles |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Serif** (default) | Hoefler Text, as now | ships with iOS | all four |
| **Sans serif** | SF Pro, the iPhone's own | ships with iOS | all four |
| **Typewriter** (monospaced) | IBM Plex Mono | OFL, bundled | all four |
| **Comic** (readable, for dyslexic readers) | Comic Neue, a free redesign of Comic Sans | OFL, bundled | all four |

- **Glyph coverage, checked 10 October 2026** (every character in Divinum Officium's Latin
  and English trees and the Ambrosian data, against each font's character map):
  - **Courier Prime** lacks *ǽ* and *Ǽ* (as in *sǽcula*, on almost every page) and the
    combining accent, so iOS would draw every *ǽ* in another typeface. **IBM Plex Mono**
    (OFL) has every Latin character the office uses and replaces it.
  - **Comic Neue** lacks *ǽ* and *Ǽ* but has the combining accent: with it, the typesetter
    writes *ǽ* as *æ* + U+0301, which the font draws itself. A few macrons in the English
    (*ē*, *ō*) fall back to the system font.
  - No font has ✠ or ✙: iOS draws the red crosses from its symbol font, as it does today
    with Hoefler Text.
- **Glyph coverage first.** Before a font goes in, CI renders every character the bundle
  uses (æ œ, every accented vowel, *ǽ*, ℣ ℟ where they appear, ✠ ✙, the asterisk) in each
  style. A font with a missing glyph is left out, or its gaps listed for the user.
- **Size.** Typefaces differ in how large they look at the same point size. Each gets one
  scale factor so its body text matches Hoefler Text's x-height at the default size; the
  text-size setting scales on top, and the line pitch and page breaks follow, as now.
- **Size of the app.** Two bundled families add well under 1 MB; reported in the PR.
- **The chrome** (navigation title, date line, footer) stays SF in every font choice.

## 3. The themes

Five colours per theme, as now (`Theme.swift`): background, text (with headings, day title
and rules), rubric (with the date line), icon (the table-of-contents button) and chrome.
The contrast of each against its background is at least 4.5:1 (WCAG AA for body text):

| Theme | Meaning | Background | Text | Rubric | Icon | Chrome | Contrast: text / rubric / chrome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nox** (default, the current look) | night | `#000000` black | `#FFFFFF` | `#FF8080` | `#FF4D33` | `#B2B2B2` | 21.0 / 8.7 / 9.9 |
| **Media nox** | midnight | `#0B1530` navy | `#FFFFFF` | `#8CB8FF` blue | `#5C9DFF` | `#98A3BC` | 18.0 / 8.9 / 7.1 |
| **Carbo** | charcoal | `#1C1C1E` dark grey | `#F2F2F2` | `#FF8A80` | `#FF5A45` | `#A1A1A6` | 15.2 / 7.5 / 6.6 |
| **Lux** | light | `#FFFFFF` white | `#000000` | `#B3261E` red | `#C62828` | `#6B6B6B` | 21.0 / 6.5 / 5.3 |
| **Sepia** | (Latin already: the cuttlefish whose ink gave the colour) | `#F3E9D2` | `#3A2E22` brown | `#9E2A1E` | `#A8321F` | `#6B5A45` | 10.9 / 6.2 / 5.5 |
| **Vellum** | (the user's choice over *Membrana*) | `#FBF7EE` cream | `#000000` | `#8B1A1A` deep red | `#A61E1E` | `#6E675C` | 19.6 / 8.7 / 5.2 |
| **Silva** | forest | `#F2F7EE` off-white green | `#000000` | `#1B5E20` dark green | `#1B5E20` | `#4D5E4F` | 19.3 / 7.2 / 6.4 |
| **Rosa** | rose, the colour of *Gaudete* and *Lætare* | `#FCF1F5` off-white pink | `#000000` | `#9C1352` magenta | `#B0175E` | `#6B5560` | 19.0 / 7.2 / 6.2 |

Three dark themes and five light ones. *Rosa* is the slightly crazy one: the rose of the
two Sundays when the Church wears pink. The English names are not shown; the Latin ones
are. The code keeps English identifiers (`classicDark`, `midnightBlue`…), as `CLAUDE.md`
asks.

- **Light themes** switch the system parts too (status bar, Settings, the date picker, the
  hour picker) to light, by the theme's own colour scheme instead of today's fixed
  `.preferredColorScheme(.dark)`.
- **The calendar's colour dots**: a white day's dot is invisible on a white page, so on
  light themes each dot gets a thin outline in the chrome colour.
- **The app icon** isn't tied to the theme; it stays its own choice.
- **The page curl** shows the page's back in the theme's background.

## 4. The galleries

Settings gets **Theme** and **Font** rows, each showing the current choice, like the *App
icon* row, and opening a page laid out like `AppIconPicker`'s: a two-column grid of tiles,
all visible at once, the name under each, and the current one ringed with a checkmark;
tapping a tile chooses it. The eight themes fill four rows, each named in Latin.

Each tile is a small page in that theme's background, rounded like an icon tile, set by
the office's own typesetter (`OfficeTypesetter`) so it shows exactly what the office will
look like:

> *Ant.* (a fixed antiphon) in bold italic
> **Magníficat ✠ \* ánima mea Dóminum.** with its red cross, then the next verse in italic

The text comes from the bundled data (the Vulgate *Magnificat*, Divinum Officium's
`Psalm232`, after J→I), not typed into the app. The Theme page shows the tiles in the
current font; the Font page shows them in the current theme.
