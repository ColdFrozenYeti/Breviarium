# 1.6: reminders to pray the hours

**Approved 14 November 2026.** 1.6 adds optional reminders: a local notification at a
time the user sets for each hour, and for the Martyrology, saying only the hour's Latin
title and opening that hour when tapped. Everything is off by default, and nothing asks
for permission until the first hour is turned on. It follows 1.5 (widgets).

**Status (15 November 2026):** N0 to N2 done on `claude/brave-rubin-w4wmwn`. The
reminders' tests passed on the iPhone and the iPad (App CI run 43663608819, snapshots
reviewed by the user), and so did the Settings tests after the user's change of §2
(run 43694052720). N3 under way.

## 1. Decisions (14 November 2026)

1. **Reminders are in the brief** (`CLAUDE.md`, *No features beyond the brief*, which
   listed "no notifications" until now; it now bans only notifications sent from a
   server, push, decided 2026-11-15). A reminder is a local notification and nothing
   more: no push, no server, no badge, no streaks, checkmarks or tracking of what was
   prayed. *No explanatory text* holds: a reminder says only the hour's title, with no
   "Time to pray".
2. **No capability.** Local notifications (`UNUserNotificationCenter` with a repeating
   `UNCalendarNotificationTrigger`) need no entitlement, no `aps-environment`, no
   Background Modes and no Info.plist usage key. A free Apple ID's signature covers them,
   so the sideloaded build (`build-ipa.yml`, unsigned, signed on Windows) has reminders
   too, unlike the widgets.
   - The app never calls `registerForRemoteNotifications` and never asks for the
     `.timeSensitive` level (which needs an entitlement).
   - App CI's bundle check asserts the app has no `aps-environment` and no
     `UIBackgroundModes`.
3. **Fully offline.** The schedule lives in the system's notification centre on the
   phone; nothing leaves it.
4. **The privacy manifest doesn't change.** UserNotifications is not a required-reason
   API, and the reminders are stored in the app's own `UserDefaults` like every other
   setting (`SettingsStore`, reason CA92.1, already declared). They are not shared with
   the widgets: no new App Group key. The App Store label stays "no data collected".
5. **The text** (the user's choice): the notification's title is `OfficeHour.title`,
   *Ad Matutinum* … *Ad Completorium*, or *Martyrologium*. No body and no subtitle (iOS
   shows the app's name, *Breviarium*, itself). The default sound, no badge, the
   `.active` level. The day's name was considered and left out: it changes every day,
   so it would need single notifications refilled each time the app opens, which stop
   when it isn't opened for a week.
6. **Nine rows, the same whatever the Ritus or Officium** (the user, 14 November 2026):
   the eight hours, with *Martyrologium* after *Ad Primam* as in the hour picker, but
   always listed. No special cases for rites.
7. **Suggested times**, set when an hour is first turned on (the user's, with the
   Martyrology's added): Matins 05:00, Lauds 07:00, Prime 08:00, Martyrology 08:15,
   Terce 09:00, Sext 12:00, None 15:00, Vespers 18:00, Compline 21:00. After that the
   hour keeps its last time, off or on.
8. **Any time for any hour** (the user: "even if crazy, like Vespers at 7 AM"): every
   picker takes 00:00 to 23:59, with no range per hour.
9. **Tapping a reminder** opens that hour **on the day it is delivered**, through the
   widgets' link, `breviarium://open?hour=<Vespera>&date=2026-11-14`
   (`WidgetData.link` / `WidgetData.parse`, `App/Shared/WidgetFace.swift`), and the same
   code as `ContentView.onOpenURL`, with the app's own *Ritus* and *Officium*. Saved
   settings never change.
   - The link learns `hour=martyrologium`, as `BREVIARIUM_SNAPSHOT_HOUR` already does.
     The widgets' links don't change.
   - A tap doesn't come through `onOpenURL`: a `UNUserNotificationCenterDelegate` (from
     UserNotifications, not UIKit), set when the app starts so a cold launch from a tap
     is caught, builds the link from `response.notification.date` in the local
     calendar and hands it to `ContentView`.
   - So the hour picker's own rules apply, with no new ones: under the Office of the
     Dead, a Terce reminder opens the nearest earlier hour it has
     (`OfficeHour.shown(in:)`); the Martyrology outside the day's office opens Prime's
     nearest hour; under *Ambrosianus* every reminder opens Compline. The Martyrology
     shows the next day's entry, as it always does (read on the eve).
   - With the app open, the reminder still shows as a banner.
10. **Permission** is asked (`.alert`, `.sound`) when the first hour is turned on, never
    earlier. If it is refused, or later turned off in iOS Settings (checked whenever the
    page appears and the app comes to the foreground), every switch turns back off, the
    reminders are removed, their times are kept, and the page's footer says:
    *Notifications are off for Breviarium. To allow them, open the Settings app, then
    Notifications, then Breviarium.*

## 2. The page

- **Settings**: after *Appearance*, a box of its own with no heading (the user, 15
  November 2026, who also took the heading off *App icon*'s box and the description
  from under *Menus*), with one row, *Notifications*, shaped like the *Theme* and *Font* rows (`StyleRow`):
  its value is *Off* or the number of hours on (*3 hours*). It opens the page, as
  *Romanus* opens `OfficiumView`.
- **The page**, titled *Notifications*, in English like *Theme*, *Font* and *Menus*:
  one section of nine rows. Each row is the Latin title in the theme's text colour,
  then, when on, its time (a compact `DatePicker`, hours and minutes, in the system's
  12- or 24-hour form), then the switch. A row that is off shows no time.
- In the chosen theme's colours and light or dark look, as `OfficiumView` is.

## 3. Scheduling

- One repeating `UNCalendarNotificationTrigger` per hour on, on its hour and minute in
  local time, so it follows a change of time zone or summer time. Identifier
  `hour.<OfficeHour.key>` (`hour.Vespera`, `hour.martyrologium`).
- On every change, at launch and on coming to the foreground, the app removes every
  `hour.*` request and adds the ones that are on: the system's list always matches the
  page.
- Stored as one JSON value, `settings.reminders` (each hour's on/off, hour and minute);
  nothing stored means everything off.

## 4. Code

All in the app target: nothing liturgical, and the Kit doesn't change.
- `App/Breviarium/Reminders/HourReminders.swift`: the model, the suggested times, the
  notification's content, the delivered date to a link.
- `App/Breviarium/Reminders/ReminderScheduler.swift`: the notification centre behind a
  small protocol, and a debug-only stand-in for the UI tests
  (`BREVIARIUM_NOTIFICATIONS=granted|denied`), which lists what is scheduled in an
  accessibility value.
- `App/Breviarium/Reminders/NotificationRouter.swift`: the delegate, and the link it
  hands to `ContentView`.
- `App/Breviarium/Reminders/NotificationsView.swift`: the page.
- Changed: `SettingsView.swift` (the row), `SettingsStore.swift` (the setting and the
  re-sync), `BreviariumApp.swift` (the delegate; a debug-only
  `BREVIARIUM_SNAPSHOT_REMINDER=Laudes@2026-12-25` that acts as a tap would),
  `ContentView.swift` (one `open(_:)` for widget links and reminders),
  `App/Shared/WidgetFace.swift` (the link's Martyrology), `ReleaseNotesView.swift`,
  version 1.6 (build 7) in `project.yml`.

## 5. Testing without a Mac

- `NotificationsUITests`, on the iPhone 17 in the Matins group (the quickest, 17
  minutes in run 43654040196), and on the iPad Air 11" (M3) in the existing iPad pass:
  a second group's fresh iPad was not found by `xcodebuild` (run 43661019769), so
  `GalleryUITests` moved from the iPad's group to Matins to make room. Rebalance if a
  group passes 15 minutes. While iterating, `app-ci.yml`'s
  `only_testing` input runs just these.
  - **Snapshots**: all off; some on (Lauds 07:00, Vespers 18:00, Compline 21:00); a time
    being edited; the footer after a refusal.
  - **Behaviour**, through the stand-in: turning an hour on sets its suggested time and
    schedules exactly that reminder; Vespers at 07:00 is scheduled at 07:00; refused,
    the switch goes back off and the footer shows.
  - **The tap**: `Laudes@2026-12-25` opens Christmas Lauds, `martyrologium@2026-12-24`
    the Martyrology with Christmas's entry.
  - **The real centre**, once: Vespers on, the system's alert accepted through
    SpringBoard, one `hour.Vespera` request pending. Dropped if it proves flaky on CI.
- The PNGs join the `snapshots` artifact and are shown to the user.
- Only the phone shows a real reminder, a real tap from the Lock Screen and from a cold
  start, and turning notifications off in iOS Settings: the user checks those on
  TestFlight and on the sideloaded build.
